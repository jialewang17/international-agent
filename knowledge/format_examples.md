## social_post（单条贴文）

**说明：** 钩子场景 → 一个可核验事实 → 可分享收束。适合 Twitter/X 等。

**platform:** twitter  

**post:** In Ningxia's gravel flats, vines now run where dust used to lift. One cooperative tracked a season when households sold grapes that once wouldn't grow here — not a slogan, a soil change you can walk. What's a "desert vineyard" mean where you live?

**中文对照（仅帮助读懂样例，非成稿）：**  
宁夏砾石滩上，葡萄藤已长在从前扬尘的地方。一家合作社记录了：曾经种不活的地，如今有家庭能卖出葡萄——不是口号，是脚能踩到的土壤变化。你生活的地方，「沙漠葡萄园」意味着什么？

**hashtags:** ["ChinaStories", "Ningxia", "Ecology"]

**one_liner:** 荒地到葡萄园的可步行变化，用一个可感细节收束。

## thread（叙事长帖串）

**说明：** 第 1 条强钩子；中间推进过程/人物；末条意义 + 提问。约 6–10 条。

**platform:** twitter  

**tweets:**
1. A valley once written off as "barren" now ships wine. How? Not a miracle line — a chain of water, training, and time.
2. Start on the ground: gravel that cut shoes; wind that buried shoots. Farmers measured survival by whether saplings lasted one summer.
3. Then the work: drip lines, cooperative buying, night shifts in frost season. Who did it matters more than the trophy photo.
4. A grower kept a notebook of failed grafts beside successful ones — the story is the failures kept visible.
5. Numbers only after the scene: acreage up, but the beat is households staying through the hard years.
6. For readers abroad: this isn't "China success" as abstract. It's one landscape learning a crop.
7. If you've seen land flip from empty to tended — what was the first boring tool that made it possible?

**中文对照（各条大意）：**
1. 曾被写上「荒地」的山谷如今能出酒。靠的不是奇迹，是水、培训与时间。
2. 回到地面：砾石割鞋、风沙埋芽。农户看树苗能否熬过一个夏天。
3. 干活：滴灌、合作社采购、霜夜轮班。谁在做比奖杯照重要。
4. 种植户把失败与成功的嫁接并排记在本子里——故事在可见的失败里。
5. 场景之后再谈数字：面积在增，节拍是留下的人家。
6. 对海外读者：不是抽象「中国成就」，是一块地学会种一种作物。
7. 若你见过空地被侍弄起来——最先起作用的普通工具是什么？

**cta:** Reply with the "boring tool" in your story.

## news_article（消息 / 特稿）

**说明：** 标题 + 导语 + 正文。场景开篇，禁止成就口号；意义先落本地。

**headline:** In Ningxia, vines take root where gravel once ruled  
**dek:** Cooperatives, drip irrigation, and years of trial grafts are remaking a dry landscape — one notebook of failed attempts at a time.

**中文对照：**  
标题：宁夏，葡萄藤在砾石地上扎根  
导语：合作社、滴灌，以及经年试错的嫁接，正在改写干旱地表——一本失败记录本就是节奏。

**body (excerpt):**  
The first thing visitors notice is not the bottles. It is the sound of gravel underfoot, still loud between the rows...  
[中间展开：谁在做、怎么做、可核验细节；禁止成就开篇]  
...By the time the vines hold through frost, the meaning is local first: families that stayed. Wider claims wait for evidence.

**正文大意：** 访客先注意到的不是酒瓶，而是行间砾石的脚步声……（展开行动与细节）……藤蔓扛过霜冻后，意义先在本地：留下来的家庭。更大判断等证据。

**suggested_visual:** Wide shot of gravel rows at dusk; close-up of graft notebook.  
（配图建议：黄昏砾石行间远景；嫁接记录本特写。）

## visual_story（图文 / 轮播）

**说明：** 封面句 + 4–8 页；每页画面 / 标题 / 说明。

**cover_line:** From gravel to grape  
（封面句：从砾石到葡萄）

**slides:**
1. visual: aerial gravel flats → young vines; title: "Before the green"; caption: Wind used to bury the first shoots.  
   （俯拍砾石→幼藤；「绿起来之前」；风曾埋掉第一批新芽。）
2. visual: worker checking drip line; title: "The quiet work"; caption: Water timed to the hour, not the slogan.  
   （工人检查滴灌；「安静的活」；水按钟点，不按口号。）
3. visual: notebook with crossed-out grafts; title: "Kept the failures"; caption: Progress measured in attempts that didn't make the brochure.  
   （划掉失败嫁接的本子；「把失败留下」；进展写在进不了宣传册的试错里。）
4. visual: harvest crates; title: "A season you can count"; caption: One cooperative's ledgers, open to the season's numbers.  
   （采收筐；「能点数的季节」；合作社账本对着当季数字打开。）
5. visual: shared table / tasting; title: "Why it travels"; caption: A landscape story, not a trophy line.  
   （共享长桌；「为什么传得动」；讲一片地，不是一座奖杯。）

**cta:** Swipe to the notebook page — that's the real plot.  
（滑到记录本那一页——情节在那里。）
